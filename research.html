Research interests

My research interests include sequential prediction, world models, and agents for long-horizon planning.

Neural weather forecasting

My bachelor’s thesis studied whether adding physical modules to neural forecasting models improves short-term weather prediction. We proposed two novel hybrid architectures, PredFormerGFT and PI-IAM4VP, and evaluated them on WeatherBench against the original architectures and other baselines, demonstrating improved forecasting performance.

My bachelor’s thesis was carried out with research guidance from Fedor Ratnikov and Denis Derkach, with day-to-day supervision by Fedor Buzaev.
